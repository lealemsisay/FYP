## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an atmosphere of collegiate prestige, intellectual authority, and modern technological precision. Created for an AI-assisted scholarship discovery platform, the visual language draws inspiration from classical collegiate libraries, archival manuscripts, and stately university grounds, filtered through a sharp, contemporary digital product architecture.

### Emotional Response & Principles
- **Scholarly Authority:** Evokes institutional credibility, trust, and enduring legacy through measured composition, editorial serif headlines, and rich botanic undertones.
- **Optimism & Belonging:** Replaces the anxiety of funding discovery with calm, dignified clarity. The interface feels welcoming yet distinctly elevated.
- **Algorithmic Clarity:** Balances human warmth with technical intelligence, presenting complex multi-parameter evaluation algorithms through clear data-density and distinct visual semantics.

### Design Movement
The interface operates under a **Modern Academic Editorial** paradigm: structured grids, generous margins, delicate rules, and refined typographic contrast juxtaposed with restrained modern container tiers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, heritage-grounded atmosphere centered around `#1F3A30` (Deep Ivy Forest), energized by sharp luminous accents and seated on warm architectural neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1F3A30`):** Deep Ivy Forest. Conveys institutional heritage, grounding navigation bars, primary calls to action, high-level headers, and authoritative framing.
- **Secondary (`#14B8A6`):** Vibrant Academic Teal. Denotes AI semantic matches, computational inference, intelligent sorting indicators, and interactive focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`):** Endowment Gold. Reserved strictly for prestigious endowments, merit ribbons, high-value opportunities, bookmarked achievements, and pending requirement states.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1F2937`):** Deep Charcoal. Delivers high-legibility typographic rendering without the stark harshness of absolute black.
- **Neutral Surfaces:**
  - Canvas Base: `#F8F6EF` (Warm Architectural Parchment)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Paper)
  - Surface Muted / Inset: `#F2EFE5` (Linen Neutral)
  - Architectural Borders: `#E5E1D4` (Warm Chalk Rule)

### Dual-Layer Evaluation Semantics
1. **AI Semantic Relevance Engine:** Represented by Secondary Teal (`#14B8A6`) to Emerald glows and badges, communicating predictive contextual fit.
2. **Deterministic Rule Verification:**
   - **Satisfied:** `#0B6B4F` (Forest Green checkmarks and subtle soft fills).
   - **Unmet / Action Needed:** `#BE123C` (Rich Crimson/Rose indicating strict requirement deficiencies).
   - **Ambiguous / Missing Documentation:** `#D4AF37` (Endowment Gold).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the classical, literary grace of **EB Garamond** with the clean, pragmatic legibility of **DM Sans**.

### Roles & Structural Balance
- **EB Garamond:** Deployed for high-level narrative orientation, hero statements, scholarship program titles, and institution names. It conveys weight, tradition, and scholarly prestige.
- **DM Sans:** Acts as the utilitarian engine across scholarship data points, complex filter matrices, body descriptions, and interactive elements. Its geometric clarity prevents fatigue in data-heavy views.
- **Metrics & Figures:** Numeric scholarship amounts (e.g., "$45,000 / Year") are rendered in EB Garamond with medium weight to look akin to classical endowment charters, paired with DM Sans micro-labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural symmetry and generous margins of classical academic journals and library reading rooms.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop Grid:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container boundary of `1280px` for readability, flanked by `3.5rem` minimum page margins.
- **Two-Panel Scholarship Explorer:** Employs an asymmetrical 4:8 split layout on desktop screens (33% persistent filters and profile strength dashboard, 66% scholarship feed and contextual drawer).
- **Rhythm & Proportions:** Vertical rhythm is calculated in multiples of `0.5rem` (`8px`). Dense data blocks utilize `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) between adjacent tags, while cards and sections receive deliberate separation using `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) to eliminate clutter and cognitive strain.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of a tactile, paper-stack approach rooted in bookbinding and fine archival paper.

### Depth Stratification
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8F6EF` provides an ambient, warm physical foundation.
- **Structural Elevation (Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Surface):** Interactive elements sit upon pure `#FFFFFF` cards, framed by crisp `1px` borders in `#E5E1D4`. 
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - **Level 1 (Resting Cards):** `0px 1px 3px rgba(31, 58, 48, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(31, 58, 48, 0.02)`. Shadows are explicitly tinted with the primary ivy undertone rather than neutral gray.
  - **Level 2 (Hover & Active Contexts):** `0px 4px 8px rgba(31, 58, 48, 0.06), 0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(31, 58, 48, 0.08)`, combined with a border transition to `#1F3A30`.
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `0px 16px 36px -8px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.14)`. Backdrop filter applies `blur(4px)` with a `#1F3A30` overlay at 18% opacity.
- **AI Highlight Elevation:** Match score modules carry a diffuse radial aura: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

In alignment with the Soft (`1`) roundedness scale, structural forms carry a tailored corner radius (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) that evokes trimmed stock paper, archival folders, and collegiate bookplates.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Base Components:** Input fields, buttons, and alert strips utilize `0.25rem` (`rounded`) to preserve architectural rigor.
- **Cards & Surfaces:** Container panels and scholarship cards employ `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) for a composed, refined finish.
- **Pill Badges:** Metadata chips, status indicators, and AI match tags use full pill borders (`rounded-full`) to contrast against the rectilinear paper framework of the layout.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Ivy (`#1F3A30`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, subtle `0.5px` border in `#1F3A30`. Hover state lightens to `#26493D` with an Ivy-tinted shadow.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base, `#1F3A30` label, `1px` border in `#E5E1D4`. On hover, background shifts to `#F2EFE5` and border darkens to `#1F3A30`.
- **Tertiary / Discovery Action:** Subtle `#14B8A6` tinted background (10% opacity) with `#0B6B4F` label and crisp teal border. Used exclusively for AI recommendation deep-dives.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **AI Relevance Score:** Pill-shaped badge with `#14B8A6` stroke, `#14B8A6` at 8% background fill, featuring bold DM Sans tabular figures paired with a subtle teal radiance.
- **Deterministic Requirement Statuses:**
  - *Satisfied:* Soft cream-green fill (`#E8F5E9`), dark ivy text (`#0B6B4F`), verified checkmark icon.
  - *Unmet:* Soft rose fill (`#FFE4E6`), deep crimson text (`#BE123C`), micro action icon.
  - *Missing Info:* Warm gold fill (`#FEF3C7`), bronze-gold text (`#92400E`), alert indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E1D4`, padding `space-sm space-md`, text `#1F2937`. Focus shifts the border to `#1F3A30` with an ambient `3px` focus ring in `rgba(31, 58, 48, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and circular controls with custom `#1F3A30` active state, marked with crisp white geometric ticks.

### Cards & Scholarship Modules
- **Scholarship Overview Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E5E1D4` structural rule. Top header features the scholarship title in EB Garamond, accompanied by institution avatar and award amount. Lower footer isolates the AI match indicator on the left and deterministic rule fulfillment counts on the right.
- **Profile Strength Indicator:** Segmented progression gauge utilizing collegiate green for completed criteria, gold for active scholarship matches, and muted paper for incomplete parameters.
