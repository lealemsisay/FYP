Programme Overview

Editorial Abstract

The Helmut-Schmidt-Programme, established under the auspices of the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD), is designed for high-achieving university graduates from developing countries who demonstrate the civic potential to assume pivotal leadership roles in social, political, and economic modernization.

Scholars are integrated into interdisciplinary, internationally renowned Master’s degree courses focusing on public policy, governance, peace studies, and international economics across distinguished German higher education institutions. Instruction is conducted predominantly in English or German, supplemented by an intensive language colloquium prior to enrollment.
